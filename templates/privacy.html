Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as device and browser type, to help us maintain and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide core Spendly features, such as logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and keeping your account secure. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand usage patterns and improve the application. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our active systems within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting or email delivery, and these providers only process data as needed to perform those services on our behalf. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or other third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided within the app.
